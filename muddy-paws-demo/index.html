What is included in a full groom

Every dog receives individual, one-on-one attention inside our heated, fully self-contained mobile van. Here is what is included in every session:

  • Warm bath: Gentle washing tailored to coat type
  • Blow dry: High-velocity cabinet-free drying
  • Full brush out: Removing tangles and loose undercoat
  • Nail clip: Trimmed safely to a comfortable length
  • Ear clean: Gently checked and cleaned
  • Breed styling: Styled to suit your dog's breed or preference
